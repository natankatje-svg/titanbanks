# Claim Compliance Checklist

Before approval, check:

- No airplane, airport, flight, TSA, or carry-on messaging.
- No charging speed claim unless wattage is confirmed.
- No Micro-USB output claim unless confirmed.
- No “most powerful power bank ever.”
- No “charge all your devices.”
- No fake scarcity.
- No urgency timers.
- No free-shipping urgency.
- No unconfirmed specs.
- Product claims are based on approved claims only.
