# Brand Consistency Checklist

Before approval, check:

- Does it feel premium?
- Does it feel dark, cinematic, minimal, and precise?
- Is orange used sparingly?
- Is the layout spacious?
- Is it not cheap, loud, or overdesigned?
- Does it feel closer to Nothing / Bang & Olufsen than Razer?
- Is there no RGB?
- Is there no neon overload?
- Is there no dropshipping aesthetic?
- Is there no urgency timer?
- Is there no stock-photo feeling?
- Does it communicate control, calm, readiness, and premium utility?
