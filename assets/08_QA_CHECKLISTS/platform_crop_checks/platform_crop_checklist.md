# Platform Crop Checklist

Before approval, check:

- Meta Feed 4:5 crop is safe.
- Instagram Story 9:16 safe zones are respected.
- TikTok 9:16 UI safe zones are respected.
- Text is not too close to the edges.
- Product is clear on mobile.
- Hook is visible within the first glance.
- CTA, if used, is not blocked by platform UI.
