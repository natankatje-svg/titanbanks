# Product Accuracy Checklist

Before approval, check:

- Does it look like Titan X?
- Are there no fake ports?
- Are there no fake cables?
- Are there no fake buttons?
- Are there no fake screens?
- Are there no fake labels?
- Is the logo spelled exactly TITANBANKS?
- Does the orange woven loop match the references?
- Does the LED percentage display match the references?
- Are both short sides avoided in the same view?
- Is the product geometry realistic?
- Is no airplane or airport use case shown?
- Is no charging speed claimed unless confirmed?
