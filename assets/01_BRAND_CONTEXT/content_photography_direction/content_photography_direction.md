# Content and Photography Direction

All photography and AI-generated visuals should feel art-directed and intentional.

The product should be naturally present, not forced.

Never show:
- Someone panicking over an empty battery
- Cheap stock lifestyle imagery
- Fake overexcited reactions
- Generic gadget advertising

Always show:
- Control
- Calm
- Readiness
- Confidence
- Premium practicality

Must-Have Shot Types:
1. Hero render — product on black background
2. Hand shot — product scale clearly visible
3. Festival context — warm light, crowd blurred in background
4. Remote work context — coffee shop, laptop, Titan X
5. Strap close-up — orange woven loop, almost abstract
6. Display close-up — “100%” close-up
7. Backpack shot — loop attached to backpack outdoors
8. Road trip shot — car interior, no airport/airplane context
