# Brand Aesthetic

Titan X should feel:
- Dark
- Cinematic
- Minimal
- Precise
- Premium
- Controlled
- Quietly confident

The visual world should feel like:
- Apple keynote at midnight
- Tesla showroom with dimmed lights
- Bang & Olufsen product campaign
- Premium product photography
- A powerful object revealed in darkness

Visual Rules:
- Deep black background
- Matte black product finish
- Orange accent light used sparingly
- Rim lighting
- Controlled shadows
- Soft vignetting
- Atmospheric depth
- Premium reflections
- Sharp contact shadows
- Slow, smooth motion for video
- Spacious composition
- Minimal text

Avoid:
- RGB
- Neon overload
- Cheap gadget style
- Dropshipping aesthetics
- White-background product shots in final marketing
- Stock photos of smiling people
- Stressed person with dead phone
- Loud urgency ads
- Clutter
- Random sci-fi UI
- Fake lightning effects

Important:
White-background product shots are allowed only as product reference material.
They should not define the final marketing look.
