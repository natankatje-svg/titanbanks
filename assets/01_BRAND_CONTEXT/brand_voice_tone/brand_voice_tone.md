# Brand Voice and Tone

TitanBanks sounds:
- Controlled
- Precise
- Premium
- Calm
- Confident
- Minimal
- Direct
- Not desperate
- Not loud
- Not childish
- Not dropshipping-like

Brand Words:
- Control
- Precision
- Always on
- Premium
- No compromises
- Built to last

Approved Tone:
Short, premium, calm, confident, and restrained.

Approved Lines:
- Built for people who cannot stop.
- 50,000mAh. But that is not the point.
- Carry less. Do more.
- Every detail has a reason.
- Never at 0%.
- Engineered to outlast your day. And the next.
- The strap people ask about.
- Always at 100%.
- Festivals. Road trips. Workdays without an outlet.

Preferred CTAs:
- Claim yours
- Get the Titan X
- Own it
- See what’s inside
- Discover the build
- Coming Soon
- Join the Waitlist
- Launching Soon
- Be First to Know
- Power Is Coming
- The Drop Is Coming

Avoid:
- Buy now
- More info
- Only today
- Limited time
- Free shipping urgency
- Loud discount language
