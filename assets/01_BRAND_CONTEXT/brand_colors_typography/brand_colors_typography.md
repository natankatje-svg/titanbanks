# Brand Colors and Typography

Color Palette:
- Primary: #0A0A0A — almost black
- Surface: #1A1A1A
- Accent: #FF6B00 — orange, used sparingly
- Primary text: #F5F5F5
- Secondary text: #888888
- Borders: #2A2A2A

Optional:
- Subtle teal/cyan counter-accent
- Subtle gold edge light

Use maximum two accent colors per shot.

Typography:
- Modern sans-serif
- PP Neue Montreal
- Söhne
- Geist
- Clean geometric sans-serif
- Display font for headers
- Use two weights: 400 regular and 500 bold

Avoid:
- Playful fonts
- Gaming fonts
- Cheap tech fonts
- Distorted typography
- Overcrowded text
