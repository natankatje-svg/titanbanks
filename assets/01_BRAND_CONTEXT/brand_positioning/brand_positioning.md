# Brand Positioning

Brand:
TitanBanks

Product:
Titan X

Brand Truth:
While the world looks for an outlet, you keep going.

Core Emotion:
Control. Always prepared. Never stressed.

Brand Archetype:
The Nomad meets Silent Luxury.

Positioning:
Titan X is a premium 50,000mAh power bank built for people who cannot afford to run out of battery during work, travel, festivals, road trips, remote work, or long days away from an outlet.

Titan X does not sell just battery capacity.

Titan X sells:
- Control
- Readiness
- Freedom from outlets
- Peace of mind
- Premium utility
- Confidence when your phone hits 10%

Reference Brands:
Closer to Nothing and Bang & Olufsen than Razer.

Do not position Titan X as cheap tech, a dropshipping gadget, or loud gaming hardware.
