# Forbidden Claims

Never say:
- The most powerful power bank ever
- Buy now and get free shipping
- Charge all your devices
- Buy now
- More info
- Only today
- Limited time
- Fast charging, unless wattage is confirmed
- Flight-safe
- Airplane approved
- TSA-approved
- Perfect for flights
- Carry-on approved

Never use:
- Urgency timers
- Fake scarcity
- Loud discount messaging
- Cheap e-commerce language
- Overhyped tech claims
- Unconfirmed specs

Hard Aviation Rule:
Never market Titan X for airplane travel.

Do not use:
- Airplanes
- Airports
- Boarding gates
- Airplane seats
- Flight-safe messaging
- Carry-on approved claims
- TSA-approved claims
- Perfect for flights
- Travel by plane
