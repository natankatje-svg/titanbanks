# Target Audience

Primary Audience:
Ambitious city-based adults aged 22–34 with €30,000+ income who are constantly on the go and depend heavily on their phone and devices for work, travel, navigation, communication, content, and daily life.

Audience Profile:
- Age: 22–34
- Income: €30,000+
- Lives in or around the city
- Travels for work or pleasure
- Phone = their life
- Core fear: being at 10% battery at the wrong moment
- Values control, reliability, mobility, premium utility, and independence from outlets

Confirmed Use Cases:
- Festivals and outdoor events
- Road trips
- Remote working / coffee shops
- Long workdays without a power outlet
- Commuting
- Daily urban life

Forbidden Use Case:
Never market Titan X for airplane travel.

Titan X is 50,000mAh and exceeds the 100Wh aviation limit.
Do not use airports, airplanes, flights, carry-on luggage, TSA, flight-safe messaging, airplane seats, boarding gates, or plane travel as a use case.
