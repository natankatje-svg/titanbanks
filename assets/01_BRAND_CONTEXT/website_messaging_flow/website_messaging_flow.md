# Website Messaging Flow

Navigation:
Home · Product · Story · Reviews · Shop

Homepage Sections:

1. Hero
Purpose: Seduction
Message: Never at 0%

2. Recognition
Purpose: Recognition
Message: Festivals. Road trips. Workdays without an outlet.

3. Product
Purpose: Desire
Message: Engineered to outlast your day. And the next.

4. Strap Detail
Purpose: Differentiation
Message: The strap people ask about.

5. Reviews
Purpose: Trust
Message: Situation-based quotes, no stars.

6. CTA
Purpose: Conversion
Message: Always at 100%

Emotional Flow:
1. Stop the scroll
2. “This is for me”
3. “I want this”
4. “This is different from the rest”
5. “I trust this”
6. Conversion

Premium Conversion Rules:
- No urgency timers
- No fake scarcity
- No loud discount framing
- Use exclusivity and confidence instead
- Checkout and CTA language should feel clean and premium
