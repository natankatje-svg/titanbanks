# Creative Brief — Titan X Pre-Launch Awareness

Brand: TitanBanks
Product: Titan X powerbank, 50,000 mAh
Campaign Goal: Pre-launch awareness — build recognition, anticipation, and a waitlist before the public drop. No discount, no urgency, no purchase CTA.

---

## Strategic Frame

**Brand Truth (anchor every creative):**
While the world looks for an outlet, you keep going.

**Core Emotion:** Control. Always prepared. Never stressed.
**Archetype:** The Nomad meets Silent Luxury. Closer to Nothing and Bang & Olufsen than Razer.
**What we sell:** Control, readiness, freedom from outlets, peace of mind, premium utility — not mAh.

---

## Target Audience

Primary: Ambitious city-based adults aged 22–34, €30,000+ income, phone-dependent for work and life.
- Core fear: being at 10% battery at the wrong moment.
- Values control, reliability, mobility, premium utility, independence from outlets.

Confirmed contexts: festivals, road trips, remote work, coffee shops, long workdays, commuting, daily urban life.

**Hard exclusion:** Never airplanes, airports, TSA, carry-on, flight-safe (50,000 mAh exceeds 100Wh aviation limit).

## Main Angles (one per creative — do not stack)

Pre-launch shortlist (ordered by recommended priority):

1. **"Power Is Coming."** — Anticipation hook. Hero close-up of the carry loop or display lit. Pure tease. Best opener.
2. **"Never at 0%."** — Battery-anxiety angle. Display reading 100%, dark cinematic. Strongest emotional pull.
3. **"50,000 mAh. But that is not the point."** — Subverts spec war. Premium frame.
4. **"The strap people ask about."** — Object fetish. Macro on orange woven loop with embossed "POWER BANK".
5. **"Engineered to outlast your day. And the next."** — Endurance angle.
6. **"Carry less. Do more."** — Minimal-life angle.

Approved CTAs (pre-launch only):
- Join the Waitlist · Be First to Know · Power Is Coming · The Drop Is Coming · Coming Soon · Launching Soon

Do not use: Buy now · More info · Only today · Limited time · any % off · countdown timers.

---

## Visual Direction

- Matte black powerbank against deep near-black background (`#0A0A0A` / `#1A1A1A`).
- Single warm orange rim light or accent (`#FF6B00`) used sparingly — like an accent lamp turning on.
- Cinematic, low-key, generous negative space. Spacing is a luxury signal.
- Typography: modern sans-serif (PP Neue Montreal / Söhne / Geist). Two weights max.
- Motion: slow, fluid. Nothing pops. Everything rises.
- Reference brands: Nothing, Bang & Olufsen, Apple product page craft.

**Forbidden visuals:** white-background product shots, RGB/neon, stock smiling faces, gaming aesthetics, urgency timers, cheap dropshipping styling, hands/people (per caption library negative constraints).

---

## Product Accuracy (mandatory — pulled from `02_PRODUCT_REFERENCES/product_accuracy_rules.md` and `captions/INDEX.md`)

- Logo spelling exactly: **TITANBANKS**.
- Port-end face: 4 USB-A (one purple/blue accent) + 1 USB-C in + 1 Micro-USB in. No more, no fewer.
- Rear-end face: rectangular flashlight aperture + exactly 2 retractable cables (1 USB-C head, 1 Lightning head).
- Top face: small TITANBANKS wordmark + LED dot-matrix display window.
- Side face: power button → 4 cert icons (FCC, CE, recycle, wheelie bin) → laser-engraved spec block → small wordmark near the bottom.
- Carry loop: orange woven, "POWER BANK" embossed.
- Display readout in raw outputs: "188%" (test pattern) — hand-edit to "100%" later.
- Brick proportions match OEM aspect — no v1 stretching.
- Never show both short ends in the same frame.

---

## Claims Lock

**Approved for pre-launch:** 50,000 mAh capacity (factory certified), 4× USB-A + 1× USB-C + 1× Micro-USB, LED exact-percentage display, built-in flashlight, orange woven carry loop, matte black finish, festival/road-trip/remote-work/long-day-without-outlet contexts.

**Blocked until supplier confirmation (do NOT claim):**
- Charging speed / wattage (any number)
- Whether Micro-USB is output (currently in-only)
- Final selling price
- Bol.com positioning

**Permanently forbidden:**
- "The most powerful ever," "Charge all your devices," "Fast charging" (without confirmed wattage), any flight/airplane/TSA framing, fake scarcity, urgency timers, % off, free-shipping language.
