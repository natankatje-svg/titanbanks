# Competitor Hooks & Copy — Verbatim + Translation + Titan X Adaptation

- **Range / spec dominance** (Ads 01, 03)
- **ROI / paired benefit stats** (Ads 02, 06)
- **Promotion / urgency** (Ad 04) — DO NOT REUSE for pre-launch
- **Reframe / brand line** (Ad 05)

## Verbatim Titan X Hook Library (synthesized from approved_claims + brand_voice_tone)

Use ONE per creative. Do not stack.

**Anticipation (best for pre-launch):**
- Power Is Coming.
- The Drop Is Coming.
- Coming Soon.
- Be First to Know.

**Battery anxiety:**
- Never at 0%.
- Built for the 10% moment.
- Low battery should not decide your day.

**Capacity reframe:**
- 50,000 mAh. But that is not the point.
- Engineered to outlast your day. And the next.

**Object / craft:**
- The strap people ask about.
- Every detail has a reason.
- Carry less. Do more.

**Use case:**
- Festivals. Road trips. Workdays without an outlet.
- Built for festivals.
- Built for road trips.
- Built for long days without an outlet.

**Multi-device:**
- One power bank. Six devices.
- Charge up to 6 devices at once.
- Power for more than just your phone.

**CTAs (waitlist set only for pre-launch):**
Join the Waitlist · Be First to Know · Power Is Coming · The Drop Is Coming · Coming Soon · Launching Soon
