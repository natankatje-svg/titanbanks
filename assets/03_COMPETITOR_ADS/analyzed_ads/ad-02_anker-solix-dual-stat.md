# Ad 02 — Anker SOLIX Solarbank 4 Pro: Dual benefit-stat

Source: `raw_ads/media-7e36d8cbe160.jpg`
Format: 9:16 vertical
Brand: Anker SOLIX
Product: Solarbank 4 Pro

## 1. Visual hierarchy
1. ANKER SOLIX wordmark (top)
2. Green "NEU" badge
3. **"Anker SOLIX / Solarbank 4 Pro"** (large white product name — primary focal)
4. Product hero (mid-frame, sitting on horizon line)
5. Two big benefit stats at bottom: "Bis zu 2234€ / Einsparung pro Jahr" and "Ab 3 Jahre / Amortisationszeit"

## 2. Layout structure
Horizon-line composition. Top third = brand + product name. Middle third = product against gradient horizon. Bottom third = paired benefit stats split 50/50. No CTA chip — implicit "learn more."

## 3. Primary focal point
The product name as a wordmark moment, not the product itself. The product photo is the *evidence*; the type is the *star*.

## 4. Secondary elements
Horizon glow under product (epic landscape framing — sells "infrastructure"), the two stat blocks (rational ROI proof).

## 5. Visual flow
Top → middle → bottom split. Reader gets brand → name → "what is it" (product silhouette) → "why care" (€ stat + payback stat).

## 6. Design elements
- Black-to-deep-blue gradient floor
- Curved horizon line behind product (subtle planet/earth implication)
- Centered axis
- Blue gradient on the two bottom stats
- Generous spacing — luxury cue

## 7. Copy breakdown
- Brand: ANKER SOLIX
- Badge: NEU
- Headline: Anker SOLIX Solarbank 4 Pro (the name IS the headline)
- Stat 1: Bis zu 2234€ Einsparung pro Jahr
- Stat 2: Ab 3 Jahre Amortisationszeit

## 8. Psychological angle
Rational frame: "We don't have to convince you it's good — here's the math." Premium move because it skips persuasion and goes straight to evidence.

## 9. Why it works
- Confident silence: no headline argument, just the name + the math
- Horizon framing makes the product feel monumental
- Two stats are paired (not five) — keeps cognitive load low
- Leaves room for the audience to fill in desire

## 10. What to preserve for Titan X
- **Horizon-line product staging** — applicable to Titan X hero shots
- **Product-name-as-headline confidence** — works for "Titan X" wordmark moment
- **Paired (not stacked) supporting stats**
- **No CTA in the frame** for pure brand/teaser plays

## 11. What to adapt / reject for Titan X
- Reject the financial ROI angle (wrong category)
- Pre-launch substitute pair: "50,000 mAh / Factory certified" + "6 ports / One device"
- Or single emotional pair: "Never at 0%." / "Built for 10% moments."
- Reject blue gradient — use warm orange single accent on horizon glow
- Reject "NEU" badge — substitute small "Coming Soon" set in same restrained style
