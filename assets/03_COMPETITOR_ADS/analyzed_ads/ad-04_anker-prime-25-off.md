# Ad 04 — Anker Prime Power Bank 26K 300W: "25% Off"

Source: `raw_ads/media-9a0d91af4181.jpg`
Format: 1:1 square (Meta feed)
Brand: Anker (Prime sub-line)
Product: Anker Prime Power Bank 26K 300W — **direct category competitor**

## 1. Visual hierarchy
1. ANKER logo (top left) + "Anker Prime" search-bar lockup (top right)
2. **"25% Off"** (huge white discount headline — focal)
3. "Anker Prime Power Bank (26K, 300W)" (product name + spec)
4. Product render (off-axis, lit from front, light streak floor)
5. Cyan speech-bubble badge "May 11th – 24th Only" (urgency)

## 2. Layout structure
Top: brand bar. Middle: discount headline + product name. Lower middle: angled product render with light streak running L→R behind it. Right side: floating speech-bubble badge.

## 3. Primary focal point
"25% Off" — the discount IS the ad. Product is supporting evidence.

## 4. Secondary elements
Light streak floor (theatre lighting + dimensional depth), speech-bubble urgency badge, search-bar brand lockup (mimics e-com browsing context).

## 5. Visual flow
Brand → discount → product name → product → urgency badge. Promotion-led structure, not brand-led.

## 6. Design elements
- Deep navy-black background with horizon glow
- Sharp diagonal light streak (catwalk lighting)
- 3/4 product render with display lit at "100%"
- Cyan accents (search bar outline + speech bubble) — same brand palette as Ads 01–03
- Discount-led typography hierarchy

## 7. Copy breakdown
- Brand: ANKER · Anker Prime
- Headline: 25% Off
- Sub: Anker Prime Power Bank (26K, 300W)
- Urgency: May 11th – 24th Only

## 8. Psychological angle
Promotion-driven scarcity. Date window creates a closing door. Search-bar lockup primes the viewer for "I should look this up."

## 9. Why it works
- Discount is unmissable
- Date window adds real urgency without being tacky
- Product render quality signals legitimacy (premium 3D, not flat e-com)
- Search-bar lockup is a clever "go find this" cue

## 10. What to preserve for Titan X
- **Light-streak floor lighting** for product hero (catwalk effect — premium)
- **Off-axis 3/4 product angle** with display visible
- **Single dominant headline** at top, product as evidence below
- **Square 1:1 composition discipline** for Meta feed

## 11. What to adapt / reject for Titan X
- **Reject the entire promotion frame.** Pre-launch has no discount, no date window, no urgency badge. Brand explicitly forbids "% off," "Only today," "Limited time."
- Reject cyan/blue accents → orange single accent (rim light or thin pill border)
- Reject speech-bubble UI element entirely
- Replace headline "25% Off" with "Power Is Coming." or "Never at 0%."
- Replace urgency badge with quiet "Join the Waitlist" pill in matte black
- Keep the lighting craft and the product framing — drop the promotional skeleton
