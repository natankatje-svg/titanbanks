# Ad 01 — Anker SOLIX Solarbank 4 Pro: "Energie, die dich durch die Nacht trägt"

Source: `raw_ads/media-3fffad1244cf.jpg`
Format: 9:16 vertical (Story / Reels)
Brand: Anker SOLIX (sister-brand to Anker Prime)
Product: Solarbank 4 Pro (home battery) — adjacent category, same parent design system

## 1. Visual hierarchy
1. ANKER SOLIX wordmark (top center, small, brand anchor)
2. "Solarbank 4 Pro" + green "NEU" badge (sub-headline)
3. **"5kWh – 30kWh"** (large blue gradient headline — primary focal point)
4. "Energie, die dich durch die Nacht trägt" (supporting line)
5. Cyan-pill subscription CTA "Abonnieren = 25% Rabatt"
6. Product hero (lower 60%, blue underglow)

## 2. Layout structure
Strict top-down stack: brand → category → headline stat → emotional payoff line → CTA chip → product. Centered axis throughout. ~50/50 split between copy block (top) and product theatre (bottom).

## 3. Primary focal point
The blue gradient "5kWh – 30kWh" range stat. Largest type, highest contrast, sits at the visual rest point.

## 4. Secondary elements
Green "NEU" badge (newness signal), cyan CTA pill (action color, clearly clickable), blue floor uplight under the product (theatre lighting).

## 5. Visual flow
Z-pattern truncated to a vertical I — eye lands on brand → drops to headline → reads payoff → hits CTA → finishes on the product reveal. Product is the reward, not the hook.

## 6. Design elements
- Pure black background, soft radial vignette
- Blue→cyan gradient typography for stat
- White body copy
- Cyan pill CTA with discount %
- Stacked-module product render with directional underlight

## 7. Copy breakdown
- Brand: ANKER SOLIX
- Sub: Solarbank 4 Pro · NEU
- Headline: 5kWh – 30kWh
- Payoff: "Energie, die dich durch die Nacht trägt" (Energy that carries you through the night)
- CTA: Abonnieren = 25% Rabatt

## 8. Psychological angle
Range = scalability = "future-proof." The night metaphor sells continuity and quiet reliability — same emotional territory as Titan X's "while the world looks for an outlet, you keep going."

## 9. Why it works
- Single dominant stat (range) instead of feature list
- Emotional payoff line reframes the stat as a benefit
- Product is staged like a luxury object, not catalogued
- CTA is one decision, not a menu

## 10. What to preserve for Titan X
- **Strict vertical I-stack composition** for 9:16
- **One dominant stat or claim**, not a list
- **Emotional one-liner under the stat**
- **Product-as-reward** at bottom with directional underlight
- **Centered axis discipline**

## 11. What to adapt / reject for Titan X
- Reject blue/cyan palette → swap to warm orange `#FF6B00` accent only
- Reject "% Rabatt" CTA → swap to "Join the Waitlist" / "Power Is Coming"
- Reject the "NEU" badge style → no badges; quiet typography only
- Reject blue stat gradient → use solid premium white headline
- The night metaphor is reusable: "When everyone else hits 0%, you keep going."
