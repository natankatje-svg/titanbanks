# Ad 03 — Anker SOLIX Solarbank 4 Pro: "4 MPPT | 5000W"

Source: `raw_ads/media-80c9c09a9527.jpg`
Format: 9:16 vertical
Brand: Anker SOLIX
Product: Solarbank 4 Pro

## 1. Visual hierarchy
1. ANKER SOLIX wordmark (top)
2. "Solarbank 4 Pro" + "NEU" badge
3. **"4 MPPT | 5000W"** (huge blue gradient spec headline — focal)
4. "Bis zu 2234€ Einsparung pro Jahr" (one supporting stat)
5. Cyan-pill CTA "Abonnieren = 25% Rabatt"
6. Product flanked by two stylized solar panels at angles

## 2. Layout structure
Same vertical I-stack as Ad 01, but the bottom theatre adds two angled solar panels framing the product — symmetric V-composition. Product is anchored in a stage set, not floating.

## 3. Primary focal point
The spec headline "4 MPPT | 5000W". This is the spec-led variant of Ad 01.

## 4. Secondary elements
The two flanking solar panels (system context — shows what the product DOES, not just what it is), single ROI stat, cyan CTA.

## 5. Visual flow
Brand → spec → ROI → CTA → product staged in its ecosystem.

## 6. Design elements
- Black background
- Blue/cyan gradient spec type (largest)
- Symmetric solar-panel staging (shows context)
- Cyan CTA pill
- Identical typography system to Ads 01–02 (clear ad set)

## 7. Copy breakdown
- Brand: ANKER SOLIX
- Sub: Solarbank 4 Pro · NEU
- Headline: 4 MPPT | 5000W
- Stat: Bis zu 2234€ Einsparung pro Jahr
- CTA: Abonnieren = 25% Rabatt

## 8. Psychological angle
Spec dominance — for the audience that wants the technical headline first. Works because the spec is paired with one rational ROI line, then a clear next step.

## 9. Why it works
- Spec is large enough to be a manifesto, not a footnote
- The flanking panels turn a product shot into a system shot
- Same template as Ads 01 + 02 → audience builds brand recognition through repeated structure

## 10. What to preserve for Titan X
- **Big spec as headline** treatment is reusable for "50,000 mAh"
- **Symmetric staging** — Titan X could be flanked by 6 device silhouettes (phone, earbuds, camera, tablet, watch, drone) to dramatize "6 devices from one"
- **Template repetition across the set** — ship Titan X ads as a clear visual family

## 11. What to adapt / reject for Titan X
- The brand explicitly rejects "spec war" framing → use spec headline ONLY when paired with the "But that is not the point" reframe
- Reject blue gradient + cyan CTA → warm white headline + "Join the Waitlist" pill in matte black with thin orange border
- Reject discount CTA pattern entirely
- If using spec headline, the supporting line MUST reframe (e.g. "50,000 mAh. But that is not the point.")
