# Ad 05 — Anker SOLIX Solarbank 4 Pro: "Balkonkraftwerk neu gedacht"

Source: `raw_ads/media-a7485f1a78c3.jpg`
Format: Landscape (web banner / display)
Brand: Anker SOLIX
Product: Solarbank 4 Pro

## 1. Visual hierarchy
1. ANKER SOLIX wordmark (top left)
2. **"Solarbank 4 Pro" + "NEU"** (mid-left headline)
3. "Balkonkraftwerk neu gedacht. Und einen Schritt weiter." (sub-line, payoff)
4. Cyan-pill CTA "Abonnieren = 25% Rabatt"
5. Product render on right (mid-frame, no underlight — sits in real environment)
6. Solar panel lifestyle photography in background (low opacity)

## 2. Layout structure
Left/right split — copy block left, product right. Background is a live photographic context (balcony with solar panels) at low opacity. First "lifestyle" composition in the set.

## 3. Primary focal point
The product name + "Neu gedacht" payoff. The lifestyle context is supporting, not dominant.

## 4. Secondary elements
Background solar panel imagery (use case proof), cyan CTA, the implicit horizon.

## 5. Visual flow
Left-to-right read: brand → product name → payoff → CTA → product. Western reading flow respected. Wider format = lower density = more breathing room.

## 6. Design elements
- Photographic background at low opacity (not pure black studio)
- Same blue/cyan typography system
- Cyan pill CTA
- Product photographed in environment, not floating

## 7. Copy breakdown
- Brand: ANKER SOLIX
- Headline: Solarbank 4 Pro · NEU
- Payoff: "Balkonkraftwerk neu gedacht. Und einen Schritt weiter." (Balcony solar, reimagined. And a step further.)
- CTA: Abonnieren = 25% Rabatt

## 8. Psychological angle
Reframe ("neu gedacht" = reimagined) + progression ("einen Schritt weiter" = one step further). Sells category leadership without naming competitors.

## 9. Why it works
- Lifestyle context proves the use case without overexplaining
- Reframe language ("reimagined") is premium and confident
- Landscape format leaves more room for atmosphere — appropriate for display/web
- No spec, no number — pure brand play

## 10. What to preserve for Titan X
- **Lifestyle context at low opacity** — perfect for festival / road trip / coffee shop backgrounds with Titan X as foreground hero
- **"Reimagined / one step further" reframe pattern** → "Powerbanks, reimagined" / "Power, one step further"
- **Left/right split with product on right** as a landscape template
- **No spec, no number** — pure brand play viable for pre-launch teasers

## 11. What to adapt / reject for Titan X
- Reject solar context — substitute festival night, road-trip dashboard, coffee-shop window
- Reject cyan CTA → matte black "Join the Waitlist" pill with thin orange border
- Reject blue typography → restrained white only
- Reject "NEU" badge → "Coming Soon" set in same minimal style or no badge at all
- Background photo MUST stay low-key matte black aesthetic — no bright daylight scenes
